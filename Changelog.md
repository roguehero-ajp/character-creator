    <section>
  <h2>What My RPG Source is</h2>
  <p>
    My RPG Source is an independent character-building and rules-learning
    project for tabletop roleplaying games. Its first major tool supports
    fifth-edition Dungeons and Dragons fantasy characters using both the 2014 and 2024 rules.
  </p>
  <p>
    The builder is designed to help players create, save, print, and understand
    their characters without turning the process into a maze of mystery boxes.
    Knowledge Cards explain terms in context, while the searchable Rules Codex
    gives players a deeper place to explore.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Why it exists</h2>
  <p>
    Character creation should feel like the opening scene of an adventure,
    not a tax form wearing rusty chain mail. My RPG Source was created to make the
    process clearer, friendlier, and more useful at the table. Ultimately, the 
    end goal of this entire project is to help break down the barriers that might
    exist that stop people from ever starting to play in these magical worlds.
  </p>
  <p>
    The project is built and operated by Jason Paul in Ontario, Canada. It
    began as a hands-on creative project by a lifelong roleplaying fan who
    wanted to learn web development while building something other players
    could genuinely use.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>How character data works</h2>
  <p>
    My RPG Source does not currently require an account. Character saves are
    stored locally in the player's browser. Players may also export a JSON
    backup, import a previous backup, print a sheet, or create a PDF.
  </p>
  <p>
    Because browser storage can be cleared or lost, exported backups are
    recommended for characters you want to keep.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Where the project is going</h2>
  <p>
    The immediate focus is a dependable fifth-edition builder with strong
    support for character advancement, multiclassing, rules guidance, and
    printing. Additional tabletop systems and original tools may be added
    later as the project grows.
  </p>
</section>
