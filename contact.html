Contact My RPG Source

Questions, bug reports, accessibility feedback, privacy requests, and licensing concerns may be sent by email.

Email: myrpgsource@gmail.com

Helpful details for bug reports

  • The page and edition you were using.
  • Your browser and device type.
  • The steps that caused the problem.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, provided it contains no information you do not want to share.

Privacy and legal requests

Please use a clear subject line such as “Privacy Request,” “Copyright Concern,” or “SRD Attribution Question.” Do not send passwords, payment details, government identification, or other sensitive information.

Postal address and telephone

My RPG Source does not publish a residential mailing address or personal telephone number. Email is the official public contact method for this independent project.
