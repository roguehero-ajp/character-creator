# Johnny Muscles: Tank Thrower

Prototype development notes live alongside the game.

## Current gameplay
- Drag and release to throw the tank.
- Mutant parasite cats advance on the defense line.
- Chonkers take heavier hits.
- Combos, defense health, demo Steroids boosts, impact effects and procedural audio are active.

## Camera pass 0.2
- Maximum drag distance increased to 360 world units.
- Camera pans backward during aim for extra pull room.
- Thrown tanks can be followed forward without a camera-distance limit.
- Procedural skyline/road rendering prevents long throws from exposing empty canvas.
