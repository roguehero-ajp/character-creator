Page 1

World 4 is beaten. Rick stands over a defeated mountain lion alien and jokes, “Mountain Lion? More like Mountain Lame-o!” A tank hits the mountainside with a CRABLANK. Johnny says, “Good one, Rick Rampage!” Rick replies, “Thanks Johnny Muscles!” Johnny says, “Well it looks like these mountains are clear! We gotta find the alien base!” Rick answers, “We sure do Johnny Muscles! And I think I know just where to start! The desert!” Johnny asks, “The desert? How will we get there?” Rick lifts a car overhead and says, “By car Johnny Muscles! Hop on!”

Page 2

Johnny and Rick clasp hands in their triumphant bro shake on top of a flying blue car as it launches out of the snowy mountains toward the desert. Johnny shouts, “Yeah!” Rick shouts, “Best Friends!” The car’s license plate reads, “BROS 4 LIFE.”
