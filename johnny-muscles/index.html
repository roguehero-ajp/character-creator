Two tentacle blob aliens discover Earth. One says, “We've found what must be the dominant species! They even have their slave caretakers cleaning their poop.” The other replies, “Perfect! The military rolls in with guns and tanks blazing. A young soldier tells an old general, “General! They're impervious to our bullets!” The general replies, “This is catastrophic! We need a new strategy.” From the darkness, a voice says, “Try tanks.”

The general asks, “Who in the anabolic window are you, stranger?” The man replies, “I'm Johnny Muscles.” The general says the tanks are not working. Johnny asks, “Have you tried throwing them?” The general replies, “…uh… what?” Johnny throws a tank into an alien cat. Kaboom. Johnny says, “Bring me tanks! I've got some kitties to crush!”
