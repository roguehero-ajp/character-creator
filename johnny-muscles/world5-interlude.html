Page 1

Surrounded by alien cats, Johnny Muscles holds a tank overhead and says, “Uh-oh Rick, we’re in a pretty hot pickle now!” Rick Rampage holds up a car and replies, “I ain’t afraid of no gross alien cats, Johnny, but we could sure use a hand!” Holly Heatwave swoops in and throws thermobaric bombs into the horde. She asks, “You boys having some alien cat trouble? I don’t do kitty litter!” Johnny and Rick shout, “Holly Heatwave!” Johnny says, “Thanks for saving me and my best friend, Holly Heatwave! I’m Johnny Muscles!” Rick adds, “I’m Rick Rampage!” Holly waves, then says, “Come on! I know where the alien base is!” Johnny and Rick high-five and shout, “Yeah!”

Page 2

Holly Heatwave flies through the desert while carrying Johnny and Rick. Johnny shouts, “Flyin’ with our new best friend!” Rick shouts, “Woohoo!” Holly tells them, “You boys sure are strong!”
