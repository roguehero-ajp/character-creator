GENERAL THERESA

The Terrible Tigon who commands the battlefield itself.

Theresa stands inside the alien forward base behind a living shield. She does not chase the fight. She tells the infestation where the fight is allowed to happen.

Phase 1: break the three large glowing nodes along the near side of Theresa's shield. All three can be hit directly with a normal throw; no bank shot is required. The dotted guide shows the actual throw arc. Once every node is broken, the shield drops and Theresa becomes vulnerable. Phase 2 adds alien reinforcements. In Phase 3 she merges with the infestation and can snare your thrown tank. If she catches it, grab the tank and rip it back out of the tendril before she takes it away.
