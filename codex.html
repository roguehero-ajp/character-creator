One library, two editions

The 2014 and 2024 versions sometimes share a name while changing rules, wording, classification, or availability. Edition badges remain visible on every result so you can compare the correct version without mixing them together by accident.

This library is separate from the smaller in-builder Rules Codex. It is designed as a complete reference for the spells and magic items released under the two Creative Commons System Reference Documents.
