One library, separate rulesets

My RPG Source stores each game system and edition in its own Codex collection. Even when two editions use the same term, their entries are kept separate so later updates, comparisons, and builder links remain dependable.

The Rules collection uses original, beginner-friendly explanations. The Spells and Magic Items collections preserve licensed SRD material with visible source and edition labels.
