BRIARWELL NORTHERN OUTSKIRTS MAP ART
===================================

Canonical runtime backgrounds for F15, the Old River Bridge and Northfield are
stored beneath this directory. Every image is authored at the Avendor reference
size of 1448 x 1086.

Approved route graph:

          F16
           |
  F18 <-walking path-> F15 <-old wagon road-> F14
                         |
                  old east wagon road
                         |
                  Old River Bridge <-> Northfield
                                            |
                                      northeast trail
                                            |
                                      Misty Forest MF1

Route-art contract:

- F15 v2 has exactly four openings. South and east are straight sections of the
  quieter historic wagon road first seen on F14. West is the visibly narrower
  walking path into playable F18. North is an untracked natural gap into F16,
  with no road or path painted toward it.
- The Old River Bridge has one straight west/east wagon deck. The intact stone
  span is the only walkable way across the rapid north/south river; neither river
  bank creates another route.
- Northfield is a broad green upland dominated by rocks, with almost no trees.
  It has one western wagon approach from the bridge and one faint northeast
  walking path into playable Misty Forest MF1. No other edge is open.

The darker magical forest from F16 through F22 continues beneath the sibling
dark-forest folder. Those screens use clear vegetation gaps instead of roads.

Runtime geometry and reciprocal transitions live in avendor/data/maps.
