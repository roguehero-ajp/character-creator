BRIARWELL WEST OUTSKIRTS MAP ART
===============================

Canonical runtime backgrounds for the western farm road are stored beneath this
directory. Every image is authored at the Avendor reference size of 1448 x 1086.

Approved route graph:

  F8 <-> F11 <-> F12 <-> F13 <-> F14
          |       |       |
       Donson   Bayard  Allwood

F13 also connects east to Briarwell's west-road junction. F14 visibly continues
north toward planned F15, but that route remains unavailable until F15 is built.

Road-art contract:

- F11 and F12 each show one straight north/south wagon road and one straight west
  farm branch. Their east boundaries remain visually closed.
- F13 shows one clean four-way crossing, with exactly one road mouth per edge.
- Donson's Farm, Bayard's Ranch and Allwood's Gardens each have one straight east
  wagon lane and no other travel opening.
- F14 shows one straight north/south road. Its southern half is maintained; the
  historic northern continuation is quieter, snowier and less travelled, while
  still reading as an old wagon road rather than a footpath.

Runtime geometry and reciprocal transitions live in avendor/data/maps.
