BRIARWELL LAKE ISLAND ART
=========================

Canonical runtime backgrounds for the two Briarwell lake islands are stored
beneath this folder. Every screen is a full-resolution 1448 x 1086 WebP using
Avendor's painterly three-quarter winter camera.

APPROVED TOPOLOGY
-----------------

  HAUNTED HOUSE        BURIED TREASURE
        |                     |
  HAUNTED LANDING       LITTLE ISLAND

The two north/south island paths are active and reciprocal. There is no walking,
swimming or direct transition between the islands. Each southern landing is
reached by boat from Briarwell's docks only after the later lake-ferry story
event; until then all four maps remain directly selectable for development.

CONTENT CONTRACT
----------------

- Haunted Island has a playable landing and haunted-house exterior. The warped
  front door reserves a future house interior without inventing one here.
- Little Island has a playable landing and northern buried-treasure clearing.
  The cairn and disturbed earth mark the cache, but its reward remains undefined
  until Jay supplies its contents.
- Water surrounds both islands and is scenery, not walkable terrain.
